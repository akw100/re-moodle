# Re-Moodle privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Re-Moodle is a browser extension that restyles HUJI's Moodle and adds study features. It's built so that your data stays with you.

**What it stores.** Your settings and content (theme, header voice, to-dos, shortcuts, done/not-done overrides, course and block backgrounds, department colors, game high score) are saved in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage`). If you're signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs some of this between your own devices. The developer can't see it.

**What it reads.** On `moodle.huji.ac.il`, the extension reads the page and your upcoming events, through Moodle's own interface and with your existing login, only to display them restyled and to tell which work you've already submitted. On the HUJI login page it only changes the look. It never reads, stores or sends your username, password, ID number or codes.

**What it sends.** Nothing goes to the developer or to any third party. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising. When you add a shortcut, the extension fetches that one web page you chose, only to show its title and preview. Theme fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

**Your control.** Removing the extension deletes its local storage. You can also clear to-dos, shortcuts and backgrounds from inside the extension at any time.
